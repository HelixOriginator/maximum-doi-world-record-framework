Category Vision & Purpose

This category redefines the purpose of global recognition. It goes beyond quantitative metrics and traditional academic orthodoxy, bridging the gap between class-bound academic research and mass-oriented societal impact.

It is designed to protect the intellectual property of resource-constrained, unaffiliated creators across Asia, Africa and the global South. Persistent Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs) show that creative intellect belongs to humanity, not to institutional monopolies.

Why This Category Matters

Research for the masses

Governments invest billions in elite research centres, yet academia often stays confined to the "classes". Research has become focused on methodology and jargon rather than practical, human-centric impact.

Strength from adversity

Independent researchers work without salaries, grants or laboratory backing. That struggle can shape formidable policy makers and innovators whose work addresses real human needs directly.

Protection through DOIs

A DOI is an indelible, timestamped public record. It protects billions of unknown creative minds from having their ideas ignored or taken simply because they lack institutional titles.

Who This Category Serves

Grassroots creators, farmers, local problem-solvers and self-directed scholars who focus on core concepts rather than institutional gatekeeping. Approving this category would signal to unsung creators worldwide that a university affiliation is not required, and encourage grassroots patent filing, independent scholarship and democratized intellectual property protection.

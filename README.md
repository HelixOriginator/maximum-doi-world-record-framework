# Maximum DOI Research Papers by an Individual in a Year

An open-access repository and official registry framework for the proposed world record category: **"Maximum DOI Research Papers by an Individual in a Year"**.

## Overview
This repository documents and verifies **158 unique research papers** published with active Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs) across open-access repositories (including Zenodo, SSRN, and DeSci Labs) within a single calendar year (January 1, 2025 – December 31, 2025). 

This framework establishes a decentralized, open-science model to protect intellectual property rights and empower independent, grassroots, and unaffiliated creators across ASEAN, Africa, and the global South.

## Key Credentials & Metrics
* **Total Verified DOIs (2025):** 158 cryptographically timestamped records
* **National & Continental Precedent:** 
  * Certified by the **India Book of Records** (Confirmed October 8, 2026)
  * Holder of the **Asia Book of Records** title
* **Academic Impact:** Over 1,125+ SSRN downloads and 56 citations on Semantic Scholar as an independent researcher.
